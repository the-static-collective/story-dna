# Episode 000 — ATTEMPTED DELIVERY

*Candidate microfilm / script. The Department of Unclaimed Tomorrows.*

**EXT. SMALL HOUSE — SOME TUESDAY, MAYBE — MORNING**

A mailbox has a loose hinge. A woman in a badly weathered postal uniform approaches it with the determined expression of someone who has been asked to deliver the Atlantic Ocean by Thursday.

Her mailbag is larger on the inside than on the outside. This is less magical than it sounds. It is mostly paperwork.

She checks a clipboard. She checks the house number.

She checks the date.

**CARRIER**  
Oh, that's unfortunate.

She rings the bell.

A RESIDENT opens the door, still holding a spoon.

**RESIDENT**  
Can I help you?

**CARRIER**  
Only technically.

She produces a pale envelope. On its face, in elderly typewriter ink:

**CURRENT OCCUPANT**  
**SOME YEAR BEFORE THE THING HAPPENS**  
**DO NOT OPEN UNTIL POSSIBLE**

**RESIDENT**  
What's that?

**CARRIER**  
Receipt.

**RESIDENT**  
For what?

She checks the form.

**CARRIER**  
A supper. Apparently you invited six people who didn't know each other. One of them brought bread. One repaired a chair. One sat in the kitchen the whole evening because they were scared of crowds. You told them they were welcome there too.

**RESIDENT**  
I haven't done that.

**CARRIER**  
Yes. That seems to be the difficulty.

The resident peers past her at an unusual vehicle parked on the curb: a postal van with three speedometers and no license plate, only a sign reading **RETURN TO WHENEVER**.

**RESIDENT**  
Are you from the future?

**CARRIER**  
Not exactly. We're not allowed to say that. Scheduling would get involved.

She taps her clipboard, exasperated.

**CARRIER (CONT'D)**  
We deliver *possibilities*. Once they happen, somebody else's department handles the evidence.

**RESIDENT**  
So you're predicting I throw a dinner party?

**CARRIER**  
Absolutely not. I'm already in trouble for the address.

There is a pause.

**RESIDENT**  
Then why give it to me?

For the first time, the carrier softens.

**CARRIER**  
Because nobody else knew it was an option.

The resident considers this. Then opens the envelope.

Inside, a single line:

*You might want to keep an extra chair.*

**RESIDENT**  
That's it?

**CARRIER**  
Budget cuts.

She marks a box on her clipboard.

**CARRIER (CONT'D)**  
Delivery attempted. Outcome unknown. Human informed.

**RESIDENT**  
And now?

**CARRIER**  
Now you can forget it. That's still allowed.

She turns to go, then glances at the date again.

**CARRIER**  
Oh. Sorry. I'm early.

She walks toward the van.

**RESIDENT**  
Wait! What's your name?

She thinks about this longer than anyone should have to.

**CARRIER**  
It depends when you ask.

**CUT TO BLACK.**

A stamp lands across the frame:

**NO PROPHECY IMPLIED.**

*The sound of a mailbox closing. Somewhere, a chair scrapes across a floor.*

**Story DNA note:** This is an original proposed opening, not existing canonical history. The carrier remains unnamed to preserve the possibility of alternate casts, animation, game encounters, radio drama, and subsequent inventions.
